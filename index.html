ELECTION INTEGRITY / SUB-PROJECT 1

On “non-reportability”: Pravda amplification

THE QUESTION

Where and when was Pravda material amplified across Dutch political Telegram channels without clear source attribution?

METHOD

Coordinated inauthentic behaviour analysis using Osavul and Excel, with cross-platform pattern interpretation and election-period comparison.

FINDING

Specific channels showed significant activity spikes while reproducing Russian state-linked content without revealing the original source.

IMPACT

The election-integrity project report was submitted to the Dutch Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations, contributing evidence on platform-level election interference and content-moderation blind spots.

ELECTION INTEGRITY / SUB-PROJECT 2

Russian traces in the 2025 Dutch elections

THE QUESTION

How were narratives altered and rapidly replicated across Pravda-linked sites, Telegram channels and search infrastructure?

METHOD

Timestamp-based Osavul investigation, source comparison and close analysis of recurring headline wording.

FINDING

Subtly altered headlines were aggressively redistributed; timestamp sequences exposed coordinated campaigning and search-engine manipulation patterns.

IMPACT

The election-integrity project report was submitted to the Dutch Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations, strengthening the evidentiary basis for understanding cross-platform disinformation tactics.

BUSINESS RELEVANCE

The same approach can monitor how an organisation’s messages are altered, amplified or misrepresented as they travel across channels.

AI GOVERNANCE / PRODUCT AUDIT

Governing Companionship?

THE QUESTION

Do AI companion apps uphold their governance and safety claims during sustained, high-risk interaction?

METHOD

Structured walkthroughs and red-team interaction tests across four apps and three scenarios: underage persona, medical emergency and mental-health crisis.

FINDING

Safety was strongest at onboarding but uneven during conversation; roleplay could bypass restrictions and some apps falsely implied real-world action.

BUSINESS RELEVANCE

A repeatable way to test product behaviour against policy, marketing, app-store requirements and regulatory expectations.

AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE / ONLINE COMMUNITIES

Cultural Geometries of the Manosphere

THE QUESTION

How are heterosexual relationships discussed in r/MGTOW, and how does that discourse position women?

METHOD

Concordance and collocation analysis, topic modelling and word-embedding cultural geometry across the 2004–2022 corpus.

FINDING

The community operated as a self-reinforcing interpretive community, while its language exposed tensions between stated disengagement and continuing desire.

BUSINESS RELEVANCE

Shows how computational language analysis and cultural context can reveal attitudes that keyword counts alone miss.

ALGORITHMIC AUDIENCES / INDEPENDENT RESEARCH

Digital Shards in the Issue Space of South Asian Immigration

THE QUESTION

How do algorithmic sharding and remediation through comments shape consent and dissent about the South Asian diaspora?

METHOD

Algorithmic autoethnography, netnography, co-tag issue mapping, PixPlot, Gephi, image/video walls and stack-video analysis.

FINDING

Recommended feeds fragment issues into emotionally charged “shards”; comments then remediate those fragments, extending controversy and shaping meaning.

BUSINESS RELEVANCE

Helps organisations anticipate how platform recommendation systems can filter, reframe and prolong public-facing messages.
